---
title: "Create a Collection"
---

A [Collection](/api-reference/storefront/collections) is a group of products of a particular kind.

## Create a Collection

```javascript javascript
const collection = await tinyshop.collections.create({
  name: "Protein Powders",
  products: ["prod_xxxaaa", "prod_xxxbbb"],
});
```

<Note>A Product may belong to multiple Collections.</Note>

Upon creation, every collection has [a collection handle](/api-reference/storefront/collections/create) you can use for the collection's storefront page, at _/collection/:collection_handle_, for example.
