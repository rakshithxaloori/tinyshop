## Products

[Products](/api-reference/store/products) describe specific goods or services you offer to your customers. Products have Options & Variants.

[Options](/api-reference/store/options) provide the customer to choose among different [Variants](/api-reference/store/variants). For example, _Color_ and _Size_ are Options and a _Red M-sized T-shirt_ is a Variant.

Whenever a customer buys something, they buy a Variant. For example, the Product is a _T-shirt_ and the Variant the customer purchases is a _Red M-sized T-shirt_.

## Add a Variant

<Note>You can also create Variants without any Options.</Note>

<CodeGroup>
```javascript javascript
const variant = await tinyshop.variants.create({
    name: "Mango",
    options: "Flavor:Mango",    // Optional field
    active: true,
    next_refill: "",
    is_default: true,
})
````

</CodeGroup>

## Price the Variant

Variants have two kinds of prices - one-time and subscription.

<CodeGroup>
```javascript javascript
const onetime_price = await tinyshop.prices.create({
    variant: variant.id,
    active: true,
    currency: "INR",
    type: "one_time",
    unit_amount: 200000,    // 2000 INR in paise
    is_default: true,    
})
```

</CodeGroup>
